How robotic cars work

See. Think.
Move safely.

Robotic cars combine cameras, radar, LiDAR, GPS and distance sensors to understand roads, people, signs and obstacles. AI processes those signals in real time to choose a route, steer, slow down or stop.

01 / Sense
Cameras and depth sensors map the space around the car.

02 / Decide
AI identifies lanes, vehicles, people and objects, then plans a safe action.

03 / Act
Motors, brakes and steering follow the plan while the sensors keep checking.

BAG

Object awareness

How bag detection helps.

A camera recognises a bag by its shape, size and colour. Depth sensors estimate its distance and position, allowing the car to avoid it, track it, pick it up or carry it for warehouse, airport and delivery work.
