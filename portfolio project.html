Autonomous driving / field notes

How the car
sees and thinks.

Self-driving cars combine sensor fusion, machine learning and careful path planning to detect obstacles, predict movement and respond before a collision happens.

The sensors behind obstacle detection

01 / LiDAR

Laser pulses create a detailed 3D map of vehicles, pedestrians and road barriers, including in low-light conditions.

02 / Radar

Radio waves measure an object's distance and speed. Radar remains useful in fog, rain and other poor-weather conditions.

03 / Cameras

High-resolution cameras recognize traffic lights, road signs, lane markings and pedestrians through computer vision.

04 / Ultrasonic sensors

Short-range sensors detect curbs, parked cars and nearby obstacles during parking and low-speed manoeuvres.

How AI turns data into action

01 / Recognize

Computer vision classifies what the car sees, distinguishing people, cars, cyclists and road objects.

02 / Predict

Motion models estimate what nearby road users may do next, such as crossing or changing lanes.

03 / Plan

GPS, maps and traffic rules help the system choose a safe route while continually recalculating around obstacles.
